Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a Matos account?
Download the Matos app, tap "Sign Up", and follow the on-screen instructions. You can register with your email address or sign in with your existing social accounts.
How do I track my order?
Go to "My Orders" in your profile, select the order you want to track, and view real-time status updates and estimated delivery information.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept major credit and debit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and other regional payment methods depending on your location.
How do I return or refund an item?
Navigate to "My Orders", select the item you want to return, and tap "Request Return". Our team will guide you through the return process within 2 business days.
How do I enable deal alerts?
Tap the heart icon on any product to save it to your wishlist, then enable notifications in your account settings to receive alerts when the price drops.
How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. Please note that this action is irreversible and will remove all your data, order history, and saved preferences.
